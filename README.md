# Thursday 19 November 2026 — in one image

Entry for the make.fun competition
[news-image-20261119](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119):
"Sum up 19 November's world news in one image".

Referral: https://make.fun/r/nmy46sr8

## Concept

A magazine-spread front page on cream paper: a navy masthead with the date
in giant white serif, a red-italic subhead naming the three biggest stories,
a strip counting stories per region, and a "day in numbers" strip
(3 presidents · 9–6 World Court vote · $81.3bn record IPO · 109–0 Arctic
pact · 4.9% jobless · $120bn COP31 aid goal cut).

Three drawn hero scenes lead the page: Trump, Xi and Putin sharing one table
at the Shenzhen APEC summit; the World Court's 9–6 genocide ruling against
Myanmar; and the COP31 draft dropping fossil fuels. Ten more drawn scene
cards follow (Kerch Bridge, Riyadh, Cyclone Akvan, Seoul, Greenland,
Anthropic's IPO, GTA VI, Bogotá, Nairobi, Canberra), plus a small flat world
map with numbered pins and 22 one-line briefs — 35 stories in total, every
one dated to Thursday 19 November 2026.

A DATE CHECK box in the footer lists the stories deliberately left out
because they belong to other days (18 Nov: Van Gogh sale, Jeffries named for
Speaker, Voyager 1, and more; 20 Nov: COP31 close, Cyclone Akvan landfall).
Every story carries its sources and a REPORTED tag.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the image (1024×1024, PNG)
- `render.py` — draws `entry.png` from scratch with PIL
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entrypoint: copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
- `LICENSE` — MIT

Original drawings; no photographs.
